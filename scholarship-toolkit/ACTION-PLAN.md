## The single most important fact

**You do not get into UK universities by emailing them. You get in through UCAS.**
UCAS (https://www.ucas.com) is the shared application system for every UK university. One UCAS application lets you pick **up to 5 university choices**. Scholarships come *on top of* an offer — almost every full scholarship (Westminster's full ride, UCL Global, Reach Oxford) requires you to **hold an offer first**.

## Honest requirements — know these now

- **Entry route:** Nigerian WAEC/NECO alone is often NOT enough for direct Year-1 entry — many UK universities want A-levels/IB, or ask you to do a **1-year International Foundation Year** (many universities offer their own, including Westminster; foundation+degree usually still fits the scholarship rules — confirm with each university). Some Nigerian applicants with strong WAEC (very high grades incl. Maths + English) do get direct Year 1 — ask, as we did in the emails.
- **English:** IELTS Academic (~6.0–6.5 overall for CS) or TOEFL. Book early at the British Council Nigeria; the result is valid 2 years.
- **Documents:** international passport (get one NOW if you don't have one), WAEC/NECO results or predicted grades, personal statement, one reference.
- **Personal statement:** this is where you win. Tell the exact story you told in the emails — taught yourself Python/JS/HTML/CSS before AI was popular, what you built, why UK, what you'll do for Nigeria afterwards. Concrete projects beat big words.

## Money reality (even with full scholarships)

Some costs can fall on you before funding starts: IELTS (~₦300k), UCAS fee (~£28), passport, visa fee, initial flights. Universities' full scholarships usually cover tuition + living, but check whether visa/travel costs are included (Westminster's includes flights; most others don't). Keep asking each scholarship office exactly: *"What costs are NOT covered?"*
