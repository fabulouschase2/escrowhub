# UK Universities — Real Funding for a Nigerian Undergraduate (verified 9 Oct 2026)

Honest picture first: **truly full undergraduate funding in the UK is rare.** Most awards are partial (£1,000–£10,000). The big "fully funded" names you see online (Chevening, Commonwealth, Gates Cambridge) are **for master's/PhD only** — you become eligible for those later. Below is what is real right now for a bachelor's degree.

---

## Tier 1 — Genuinely full awards (tuition + living)

### 1. University of Westminster — Undergraduate Full International Scholarship ⭐ best match
- Covers: full tuition waiver **+ university accommodation + living expenses + flights**
- For: students from developing countries with a full-time UG offer at Westminster; judged on academic excellence, development potential and **financial need** (your situation fits exactly)
- You must hold an offer first → apply via UCAS, then the scholarship application
- Page: https://www.westminster.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/funding/international-student-funding
- Contact: scholarshipsteam@westminster.ac.uk (emailed 9 Oct 2026)

### 2. UCL — Global Undergraduate Scholarship
- 10 awards of **full tuition + maintenance allowance**, plus 20 awards of full tuition; need-based
- For: international (overseas-fee) students from low-income backgrounds, any UG programme incl. Computer Science
- You must apply for admission to UCL (UCAS) first; scholarship deadline has been around late April of the entry year (27 Apr 2026 for 2026 entry — expect similar for 2027)
- Page: https://www.ucl.ac.uk/scholarships/ucl-global-undergraduate-scholarship
- Contact: international@ucl.ac.uk (emailed 9 Oct 2026)

### 3. University of Oxford — Reach Oxford Scholarship
- Covers: full tuition + living-cost grant + one return airfare per year
- For: nationals of low-income countries (Nigeria is eligible) who cannot study for a degree in their own country; you must **not** have studied at university level before
- Process: apply to Oxford through UCAS → if you get an offer, submit the scholarship application form (deadline for the 2027 cycle: 26 January 2027)
- Honest note: Oxford CS entry needs very high grades + admissions tests (MAT), and the 2027 UCAS deadline (15 Oct 2026) is days away — realistically this is a target for the **2028 cycle**. Start preparing now: https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/fees-and-funding/oxford-bursaries-and-scholarships/reach-oxford
