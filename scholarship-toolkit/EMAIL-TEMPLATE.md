# Scholarship Enquiry Email — Master Template & Sent Copies

Use this for follow-ups and for new universities. Rule: **change the university name and the scholarship name every time** — generic blasts get ignored and can get your Gmail flagged.

---

## Master template

**Subject:** Enquiry about fully funded undergraduate study — Nigerian Computer Science applicant (2027 entry)

```
Dear [UNIVERSITY] [TEAM NAME],

My name is Bello Abdulrahmon. I am a 19-year-old student from Nigeria, and I
would like to ask about fully funded undergraduate scholarships for September
2027 entry in Computer Science (or a related course such as Information
Communication Engineering) at [UNIVERSITY].

I taught myself programming — Python, JavaScript, HTML and CSS — because I
genuinely love building things with code. My interest began before AI tools
became popular, and I have continued learning on my own ever since. I want to
study in the UK because [UNIVERSITY] teaches a modern, industry-connected
curriculum, while most programmes in Nigerian universities still use older
material, and because I want to learn alongside people from many different
countries.

My family cannot afford international tuition fees, so I can only take up a
place if it is fully funded. I read about [SCHOLARSHIP NAME] on your website.
Could you kindly tell me:

1. Whether [SCHOLARSHIP NAME] — or any other full or near-full scholarship —
   is open to undergraduate applicants from Nigeria for 2027 entry;
2. The academic grades, documents and deadlines required;
3. How the scholarship application works alongside my UCAS application.

I will be applying through UCAS this autumn. Thank you very much for your
time and consideration. I look forward to your reply.

Kind regards,
Bello Abdulrahmon
Email: belloabdulrahmon345@gmail.com
Nigeria
```

## When to follow up

- Wait **2–3 weeks**. If no reply, send ONE short follow-up: reply to your own sent email with "Dear team, I am following up on my enquiry below. Kind regards, Bello Abdulrahmon."
- After that, move on — do not send a third email. Use the university's web enquiry form instead.
